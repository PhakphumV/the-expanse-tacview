GUIDE & SHORTCUTS

Reading the screen

HeaderPlayback state, elapsed and remaining mission time, and the current phase (its own line, so it never pushes the clock around).
TARGETSEvery body and vehicle in the scenario. Click one to orbit or follow it; its live telemetry appears underneath.
ALT / SPDAltitude and speed. Inside the Moon's sphere of influence, SPD is lunar-relative and marked lunar; a landed vehicle reads 0.000.
POS / ΔVEarth-centred position in km, and the delta-v spent since launch.
MOONAltitude above the Moon (ON SURFACE when landed), added for cislunar targets.
TrailsVehicles leave a bright ribbon; bodies leave a dimmer one. Length is a fraction of the object's own orbit — a craft an eighth, a body almost half — and a transfer coast shows its last 60 minutes.
TimelineScrub bar with phase markers and burn bars; every marker jumps to its event. Speed goes from 0.25x to 8192x.
